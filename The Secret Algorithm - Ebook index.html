What you’ll learn

No hidden code. The “algorithm” is a loop: earn the click, keep the viewer watching, then improve.

  1. The real secret: click + watch timeThe two signals every decision should serve.
  2. Thumbnails that stop the scrollOne idea, big and bold, readable at stamp size.
  3. Titles: curiosity with clarityMake the title and thumbnail work as a team.
  4. The hook: win the first 30 secondsShow the payoff, state the stakes, skip the slow intro.
  5. Retention: keep them watchingRead your graph, find the drop, fix the pattern.
  6. Editing secrets: every second countsFast cuts, sound, zooms and B-roll.
  7. Ideas: simple, big and clearExplain it in one line, then scale it up.
  8. The loop: reinvest, measure, improvePut your gains back into better videos.
  9. Your 30-day action planStudy, plan, make, publish, learn.

Your first 30 days

Start small, ship one video, and let the data teach you.

Week 1: Study

Analyze 20 successful videos. Note thumbnails, titles and hooks.

Week 2: Plan

Write 10 ideas. Draft a thumbnail and title for the best 3.

Week 3: Make

Film one video with a strong hook and edit it tight.

Week 4: Learn

Publish, check click rate and retention, write what to improve.
